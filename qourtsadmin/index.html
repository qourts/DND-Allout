How it works

Help

Recording a match

  1. Open the match from Courts or Scores.
  2. When both teams are on court, tap Start match. The match turns Live on the board and the player portal, and Game 1 opens for scoring. Started by mistake? Undo start is there until the first point.
  3. Tap + for the team that won the rally, or tap a score and type it (handy when copying from a scoresheet). Scores save on their own within a second and appear instantly on the player portal and every other admin screen.
  4. When a game reaches 21 (win by 2), tap End game. The next game opens.
  5. When the match is decided, check the games and tap Confirm result. Standings and the bracket update from confirmed results only.

Changing courts and adding notes

  • Open the match and expand Court & notes. Pick another court from the list: courts already used at that time say busy. The court board and the player portal move the match at once, and the portal says “Moved here from Court X”.
  • If you move a match onto a busy court, the site warns you: move the other match too. Pick the court marked (scheduled) to undo a move.
  • Courts can be changed before or during a match, not after the result is confirmed.
  • Notes are for Match Control (for example a medical timeout, a ball change, a delay). They are private unless you tick Show this note to players on the portal; then the note appears on the portal’s court card and match details. Clear the text and save to remove it. Every start, move and note is in Activity.

Team match and mixed pairs

  • Every match is a team match played in order: Game 1 Women’s Doubles, Game 2 Men’s Doubles, Game 3 Mixed 1, Game 4 Mixed 2, and a Dreambreaker with all four players at 2–2.
  • The scoring screen lists all five games with the players for each one, and shows who is on court above the + buttons.
  • Under Mixed pairs, pick each team’s Mixed 1 man and woman for this match. The other man and woman play Mixed 2 automatically. It saves as soon as both are chosen. Lineups are confidential: the player portal shows each pair only once its game starts (Mixed 1 at game 3, Mixed 2 at game 4, the Dreambreaker order at game 5).
  • Dreambreaker order: at 2–2 each team gives Match Control its 1st to 4th player (any gender order). Pick them under Dreambreaker order; it saves when all four are chosen. During the Dreambreaker each player takes 4 rallies in turn, and the screen shows who is on court and who is next. The order locks once the Dreambreaker has a score.
  • Teams may change their mixed pairs every round. Set them for each match before Mixed 1 starts; after that they are locked (remove games 3 onward to change them).

Format

  • Games are to 21, win by 2. Past 21 a game ends as soon as one team leads by 2.
  • Preliminary matches play all four games (WD, MD, XD1, XD2). At 2–2 a Dreambreaker decides the match.
  • Semifinals, bronze and gold end when a team wins 3 games. At 2–2 a Dreambreaker decides the match.
  • For a knockout match, pick the two teams before the first point. The site suggests them from the standings.

Mistakes

  • Edit on a finished game corrects its score while the match is live.
  • Remove last game deletes the most recent game if it was entered by mistake.
  • A confirmed match is locked. Admins with the Correct final scores permission can Reopen it or Reset it, with a reason that is kept in the activity log.
  • If two people score the same match, the second save is refused with “changed by someone else”. The screen reloads the latest score; check it and carry on.

Forfeits

Use Forfeit on the current game to record a medical or no-show forfeit. The game is recorded as final for the other team. Repeat for each remaining game if a team forfeits the match.
